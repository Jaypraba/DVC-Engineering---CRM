Conservation area work is rarely limited by construction. It is limited by
consent, and consent is limited by how early you understand what will and will
not be granted.

## What the site visit found

The rear dormer the client wanted had been refused twice on the street in the
previous three years. Both refusals turned on the same detail. The visit
identified it, the report set out the version that had been approved four doors
down, and the application went in with that precedent attached.

## What we built

A loft conversion within the approved envelope, full re-roofing, sash window
restoration rather than replacement, and a services upgrade routed to avoid any
intervention in the original cornice work.
