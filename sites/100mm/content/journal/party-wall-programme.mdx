Most clients budget for party wall surveyors' fees and are mildly annoyed by
them. Almost nobody budgets for the programme.

## The statutory clock

Notice periods are fixed by the Party Wall etc. Act 1996 and cannot be
compressed by goodwill. Two months for work to a party structure, one month for
excavation and line of junction. That clock only starts when a valid notice is
served, and a notice served with the wrong owner named on it is not valid.

If the adjoining owner dissents — and outside of unusually friendly streets,
assume they will — surveyors are appointed and an award is drawn up. Six to ten
weeks is normal. Longer if a schedule of condition needs access that is not
being granted.

## Where the three months goes

The delay is almost never the Act. It is that notices are served after the
design is finished, because that is when the drawings that support them exist.
Design takes four months, notices go out in month five, the award lands in month
seven, and a client who expected to start in the spring starts in the autumn.

## What to do instead

Establish the party wall exposure at the very beginning, before design. You do
not need finished drawings to know that you are excavating within three metres
of a neighbour's foundations. Serve as soon as the structural strategy is fixed
rather than when the package is complete, and run the award alongside the
remaining design rather than after it.

the single most reliable way a well-run project of this size loses a season.
