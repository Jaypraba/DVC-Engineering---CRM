### Post 6: Foundation Design — Getting Depth Right in London

**Subject:** Foundation depth matters in London

London Clay is one of the most problematic soils for residential construction. It shrinks in dry conditions and swells when wet, and the root systems of nearby trees amplify this movement significantly. Getting the foundation depth right for your extension is not guesswork: it requires a structural engineer to calculate the required depth based on soil type, tree species, and tree distance.

DVC Engineering designs foundations for extensions across London, from simple strip foundations where conditions allow to deep trench fill and piled solutions where trees or ground conditions demand it. All designs are submitted with full calculations for building regulations approval.

Contact us at the start of your extension project to ensure your foundation design is correct from the outset.

**Q: What is the difference between a structural survey and a homebuyer's report?**

A: A homebuyer's report is a standardised condition assessment carried out by a building surveyor. It gives a general view of the property's condition but does not investigate structural elements in depth. A structural survey is carried out by a structural engineer and focuses specifically on the load-bearing structure: foundations, walls, floors, and roof. It identifies structural defects, assesses their cause and severity, and provides professional recommendations. DVC Engineering carries out structural surveys across London.

---

**Q: How do I know if a wall is load-bearing?**

A: The most reliable way is to commission a structural engineer to assess it. Key indicators include whether the wall runs perpendicular to the floor joists, whether it sits above a wall on the floor below, and what load exists directly above it. Do not rely on visual inspection alone: an incorrect assessment can lead to serious structural problems. DVC Engineering carries out load-bearing wall assessments and provides removal calculations across London.

---

**Q: How deep do foundations need to be for an extension in London?**

A: In areas of London Clay with no nearby trees, a minimum depth of around 1.0 metre is typically required. Where mature trees are present within influencing distance, building regulations may require foundations at 1.5 to 2.5 metres depth or deeper. The precise depth depends on the soil type, the tree species, and the distance from the extension to the tree. DVC Engineering designs foundations for all extension projects across London.

---

**Q: Do I need building regulations approval for load-bearing wall removal?**

A: Yes. Removing or forming an opening in a load-bearing wall is notifiable work under the Building Regulations. You must submit structural calculations and drawings to building control for approval before the work begins. If you proceed without approval, you may face difficulties when selling the property and may need to obtain retrospective regularisation. DVC Engineering provides structural calculations and building control coordination for load-bearing wall removal across London.
